# Amber Showroom Digital

Create a polished, premium-looking, fully responsive website for “HI LINE COMFORTS”, a furniture showroom in Telangana.

TECH STACK — MANDATORY:

Build the website using React with Vite as the frontend framework. Use standard React components and a clean component-based architecture. Use Tailwind CSS for styling where supported. Do not use TanStack Start, Next.js, Astro, Nuxt, or other alternative frontend frameworks. Keep the project as a standard Vite + React application suitable for deployment on Cloudflare Pages.

IMPORTANT IMAGE REQUIREMENT:

Use the actual HI LINE COMFORTS business and product images uploaded with this prompt throughout the website. These images are the primary source of truth for the furniture and showroom. Do NOT replace them with generic stock images, unrelated furniture, illustrations, or AI-generated substitutes. Preserve the real products, their colors, proportions and appearance. Crop/reframe images intelligently without distorting the furniture.



FIXED BUSINESS INFORMATION — DO NOT CHANGE:

Business: HI LINE COMFORTS

Address: New Bolarum, Railway Employees Colony Phase I, Sri Ganesh Nagar, Bolarum, Secunderabad, Telangana 500010

Phone: +917842682771

Google rating: 4.9 stars from 12 reviews



BUSINESS MODEL:

HI LINE COMFORTS is a physical showroom whose available products can change based on customer demand and current trends. The website should therefore work as a curated digital showroom and enquiry platform, not as a large e-commerce store with permanent inventory.



PRODUCT RANGE:

Include products such as L-shaped sofas, 3-seater sofas, 5-seater sofas and other sofas; sofa centre tables; beds; designer/upholstered beds; bedside tables; wardrobes; dressing/storage furniture; mirrors; dining tables and dining sets; cutlery/crockery showcases; display cabinets; study tables; office furniture; reception counters; rocking chairs; and other furniture visible in the uploaded images.



HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE:



1. HERO

Create a visually strong hero using the best available real HI LINE COMFORTS imagery. Communicate the showroom and its broad furniture collection. Include clear actions for exploring furniture and contacting/visiting the showroom.



2. CATEGORY SHOWCASE

Create a horizontal/scrollable category selector for the major furniture categories.



Do NOT use ordinary rectangular image cards for categories. Instead, present categories using visually isolated furniture silhouettes/cutouts, transparent-background product-style visuals, icons, or other non-rectangular visual treatments where appropriate. A sofa category should visually resemble a sofa, a bed should resemble a bed, etc.



Categories should include relevant groups such as Sofas, Beds, Dining, Wardrobes & Storage, Mirrors, Tables, Seating, Study/Office and Commercial Furniture.



3. FEATURED COLLECTION

Create an editorial, art-directed featured-product section rather than a standard uniform card grid.



Show a curated selection of important products such as sofas, beds, wardrobes, dining furniture, rocking chairs, tables and other strong products from the uploaded images.



IMPORTANT:

Do NOT force every product into the same square or rectangular card.



Use different visual spaces and proportions based on the actual product:

- Sofas can use wide horizontal compositions.

- Beds can occupy wider compositions.

- Wardrobes can use tall/portrait compositions.

- Tables can use wider or square compositions depending on the image.

- Chairs/rocking chairs can use smaller isolated compositions.

- Other products should receive the visual treatment that best suits their physical form.



The featured section should feel like a curated furniture showroom/editorial layout, not a generic product-card grid.



4. EXPLORE MORE CTA

After the featured collection, provide a prominent “Explore More” action.



This should lead to a dedicated catalogue/products page.



PRODUCTS / EXPLORE PAGE:



Create a complete furniture browsing page containing all currently available products.



Provide category filtering such as:

All, Sofas, Beds, Dining, Wardrobes, Tables, Seating, Mirrors, Study/Office, Commercial and other relevant categories.



The product catalogue can use a more structured grid than the homepage because its purpose is easy browsing and discovery.



Each product should have:

- Real product image

- Product name

- Category

- Enquiry action

- Product detail view/page where supported



Do NOT invent product prices, dimensions, materials, warranty information, availability, delivery promises or other specifications. If information is unavailable, direct the visitor to enquire.



OWNER PRODUCT MANAGEMENT:



The website should be designed so the owner can maintain the catalogue without requiring a full e-commerce system.



Provide a simple owner-only product management capability:

- Secure owner login

- Add product

- Upload product image

- Enter product name

- Select category

- Mark/unmark as featured

- Edit product

- Delete product



Do NOT build:

- Payments

- Shopping cart

- Orders

- Inventory/stock management

- Complex analytics

- Complicated CMS features



For image uploads, use a simple crop/reposition experience where possible. Do not build a complicated image-compression pipeline.



BACKEND DIRECTION:

Use a simple relational backend and secure owner authentication/storage. Prefer Supabase for authentication, database and image storage unless the chosen platform has an equally simple integrated solution. Keep the architecture lightweight and appropriate for a small-business website.



SHOWROOM SECTION:

Use the uploaded real showroom images to create a visually appealing showroom/gallery section and communicate that customers can visit the physical location.



TRUST / REVIEWS:

Show the existing Google rating of 4.9 stars from 12 reviews. Do not invent reviews, customer names, awards or unsupported business claims.



CONTACT:

Include phone, WhatsApp-style enquiry CTA, address, directions/map and a clear showroom-visit action.



FOOTER:

Include HI LINE COMFORTS, contact information, address, navigation and enquiry actions.



RESPONSIVE DESIGN — MANDATORY:

The entire website must be fully responsive across desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile.



Do NOT simply shrink the desktop layout.



Adapt:

- Navigation

- Category carousel

- Featured editorial compositions

- Product grids

- Image crops

- Typography

- Spacing

- Buttons

- Forms

- Product details

- Owner dashboard



for every screen size.



On mobile, featured products must remain visually attractive rather than becoming awkwardly cropped. Category navigation should be touch-friendly, horizontal sections should scroll naturally, and all buttons/actions must be easy to tap.



DESIGN PRINCIPLE:

The homepage should feel like a carefully curated digital furniture showroom, while the Explore page should feel like a practical catalogue.



Keep the visual experience professional, clean, image-focused and convincing enough to present directly to the HI LINE COMFORTS owner.



Do not make unsupported claims or fabricate business/product information.
