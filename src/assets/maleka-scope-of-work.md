# MALEKA FURNITURES — SCOPE OF WORK

**Date:** 7 September 2026
**Between:** Mohammed Maaz Ali ("Developer") and Maleka Furnitures, represented by Haider Ashraf ("Client")

This document lays out exactly what's included in the ₹26,000 project, what isn't, and when things are due, so both sides are working from the same understanding from day one.

---

## 1. What's Included

**Website**
- Public showroom site — homepage, offers section, category browsing, wedding-set section, product catalogue, and product detail pages
- Every product page links directly to WhatsApp and phone for enquiries
- Map, address, hours, and directions
- Fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile

**Owner Dashboard**
- A login-protected admin area, hidden from customers and not indexed by Google
- Add and edit products, and assign categories
- Image upload with fixed-ratio cropping and automatic WebP compression
- Up to 4 images per product (optional — not every product needs all 4)
- Management of active offers and promotions

**Image Storage**
- Cloudflare R2 is set up and connected as the image storage backend before any product images are uploaded — this is in place from day one, not added later.

**Product Catalogue**
- Up to 250 products, across the categories already agreed: Wedding Set, Almari, Dining, Single Bed, Dressing, Shoe Rack, Sofa, Center Table, Wardrobe, and Bister/Bedsheet Set
- Each image individually AI-enhanced — background cleanup and lighting correction — before upload

**Hosting & Domain**
- Domain purchased and registered under Maleka Furnitures' own name
- Hosted on Cloudflare Pages, managed by the Developer for the first 12 months, included in this price
- After 12 months, domain renewal (approximately ₹1,000/year) becomes the Client's responsibility, or the Developer can continue managing it as a separate arrangement

**Software Ownership**
- The website and its source code belong to the Client permanently, once payment is complete

---

## 2. What's Not Included

- Cart, checkout, or online payment — this is a showroom and enquiry site, not an online store
- Order or inventory management
- More than 250 products, or more than 4 images per product — additional products or images beyond this are a separate, additional cost
- Category-specific crop ratios — the current build uses one fixed ratio across all products
- Any changes to already-delivered work that go beyond fixing something broken (see Section 5)

---

## 3. Image Submission Timeline

The website build itself takes 1–2 weeks. The project as a whole is scoped at around one month because individually AI-enhancing 250 images is real, time-consuming work in its own right, separate from the website build — not because of any delay on the Client's side.

For that reason, all 250 products' images should reach the Developer as early as possible, ideally right away, and no later than **21 September 2026** (two weeks from project start). The website build and image processing run in parallel, so the earlier the images arrive, the more of that month is actually usable for processing them properly.

Images sent after 21 September, or beyond the 250-product / 4-image cap, are not covered under this ₹26,000. They will be handled as separate, paid update batches — ₹800–1,500 per roughly 10 images — the same rate that applies to ongoing monthly updates after launch.
